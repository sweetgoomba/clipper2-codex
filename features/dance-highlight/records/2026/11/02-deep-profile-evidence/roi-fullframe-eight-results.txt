What the images establish, separately from model conclusions
- ANT's original detected face widths are 21–32 pixels. The source crops and aligned crops show limited facial detail, soft edges, and motion/turning across the four frames. Fullframe does not reveal a different or newly sharp face; it shifts the same landmarks by about .8–1.5 pixels RMS.
- MMN has strong pink lighting in these source frames. At 38.705 and 39.106 the face is turned sideways, and the detector's inter-eye distance is only 5.9 and 2.82 pixels in fullframe. The aligned crop remains a side face with limited frontal detail. At 38.372 the face is larger (62x78 fullframe), yet dual-model agreement still fails, so small size alone does not explain the whole interval.
- Source/pose audit does not show a wrong-body association for these eight frames. The comparison rules out ROI-vs-fullframe replacement as a sufficient fix for these examples. It does not prove a single optical cause for recognition failure or prove that every timestamp in the whole interval is unrecoverable.
- Fullframe landmark changes produce substantial AuraFace vector changes (cosine distance .242–.626 from the same-frame saved vector) and SFace changes .030–.267. Because these are alternative alignments of the same pixels, they are not independent evidence. Their zero accepted matches do not justify lowering identity thresholds or choosing a rejected nearest member.
